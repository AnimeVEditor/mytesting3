Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical interior project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope. A modular kitchen might take 3-4 weeks, while a full residential turnkey interior usually takes 45 to 90 days from design approval to final handover.

Do you charge for the initial site visit?

Our initial consultation and basic site measurement within our primary service areas are generally free. Detailed 3D designs and planning commence after a project commitment.

What quality of materials do you use?

We use only premium, branded, and ISI-certified materials. Everything from plywood (BWP/BWR grade), laminates, to hardware (like Hettich/Blum) is selected for durability and a luxury finish.
